What Yeovil rooftops are usually like

Yeovil expanded substantially through the twentieth century, and a large share of its housing dates from the post-war decades. That period built flat roofs in quantity: attached garages, single storey rear and side extensions, flat-roofed dormers and porch canopies, most of them originally covered in felt.

A lot of those coverings are now well past the point where patching is worthwhile, which is why so much of our Yeovil work is a full strip and re-cover rather than a repair.

Flat roofing questions we get asked in Yeovil

Do you cover the villages around Yeovil?

Yes, the BA20, BA21 and BA22 areas generally. If you are unsure, call and we will tell you straight away whether you are in range.

Can you replace a flat roof in winter?

Yes, though the covering and the weather window matter. EPDM in particular can be laid in conditions that would be marginal for some other systems. We will not lay a roof in weather that would compromise it.

What does the 10-year guarantee actually cover?

It covers our workmanship on the roof we install. We will go through exactly what is and is not included when we give you the quote, so there are no surprises later.
