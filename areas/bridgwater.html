What Bridgwater rooftops are usually like

Bridgwater has a Georgian core, a large amount of Victorian terraced housing, and extensive post-war estates. The terraces commonly have flat-roofed rear additions and outriggers, often narrow, often overlooked, and often re-covered several times without anyone stripping back to see what is underneath.

The estate housing brings the usual garage and extension roofs, and the town's industrial and commercial stock brings larger flat roofs where the problems are typically at the outlets, upstands and edge detail.

What we get called out for in Bridgwater

  • Rear addition and outrigger roofs on Victorian terraces
  • Garage roofs on the post-war estates coming to the end of their original covering
  • Larger commercial and industrial roofs with recurring leaks at outlets and upstands
  • Flashing failures where a flat roof meets a rendered or brick wall
  • Soft or rotten decking uncovered once the old felt comes off
  • Roofline work, fascias, soffits and guttering, done alongside the roof

Flat roofing questions we get asked in Bridgwater

Can you work on a narrow rear addition roof with access problems?

Usually yes. Access is one of the things we check at the survey, because it affects both how we do the job and what it costs. If access is genuinely a problem we will tell you before we quote, not after.

Do you do industrial and commercial roofs?

Yes. Bridgwater has a lot of commercial stock and we take on units as well as houses.

How long will a new flat roof last?

It depends on the covering, the deck and the detailing. Our work carries a 10-year guarantee, and good quality GRP and EPDM systems are generally expected to run well beyond that when they are laid properly on a sound deck.
